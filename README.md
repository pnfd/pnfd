### 👨‍💻 About Me
I turn complex hardware requirements into production-ready, secure firmware. With over 16 years of experience in automotive and home automation, I specialize in bare-metal programming, RTOS architectures, and embedded security.

Currently, I am heavily focused on helping hardware manufacturers prepare for the **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)** by implementing secure connectivity solutions directly on microcontrollers.

* 🔭 **Currently working on:** Next-Gen Secure Diagnostics (UDS over VPN) & Embedded Security Audits.
* 🚀 **My Flagship Product:** A native, hardware-accelerated [WireGuard® VPN Stack for STM32H5](https://www.pnfd.de/wireguard.html) (No Linux-Gateway required!).
* 💬 **Ask me about:** C/C++, Azure RTOS (ThreadX / NetX Duo), TrustZone, Secure Boot, and Hardware Cryptography.
* 📫 **How to reach me:** [contact@pnfd.de](mailto:contact@pnfd.de)
